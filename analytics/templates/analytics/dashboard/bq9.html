Price range of each item

By condition

By seller faculty

Median price relative to the same item's median, by the faculty of the seller.

Insights

    How this is calculated

    • Prices are the asking prices of every listing (Material.price), sold or not. Free listings are left out.
    • Two listings are the same item when they share the category and the model (calculators, lab equipment), or, without a model, the title and the edition. Titles are compared ignoring case, accents and punctuation; different wordings of the same book are not matched.
    • Only items listed at least twice are compared. The spread is (highest − lowest) / median.
    • To compare conditions and faculties across items with very different prices, each listing is divided by its item's median price, and the median of those ratios is shown.
    • Completed sale prices per category and condition, used by the price suggestion, are in /analytics/price-stats/.
