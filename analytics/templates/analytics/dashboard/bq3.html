Every listing

One bubble per number of photos and number of contacts; the bigger the bubble, the more listings share it.

Insights

    How this is calculated

    • Every listing (Material) counts, whatever its status. Its photos are the length of imageUrls.
    • A contact is a buyer who opened a chat about the listing (ChatRoom). Each buyer counts once per listing, however many messages they send.
    • Listings are grouped into 0, 1, 2 and 3 or more photos. The lift is the average contacts of listings with 3+ photos divided by the average of listings with fewer.
    • The correlation is Pearson's r between photos and contacts over every listing: near 0 means no relation, near 1 means more photos go with more contacts.
    • A listing published yesterday has had less time to be contacted than one from last month, so each group also shows its average age.
    • What the photos show (angles, close-ups of damage) is not stored anywhere, so that part of the question needs image labelling and is not answered here.
