Insights

    How this is calculated

    • Course codes are compared without spaces, dashes or case, so "mate 1203" and "MATE-1203" are the same course. Listings without a course code are counted apart.
    • Supply is the listings of the course that are still AVAILABLE; reserved and sold ones are shown but not counted as stock.
    • Demand is the students interested in any listing of the course: buyers who opened a chat about it (ChatRoom) or saved it to their wishlist (WishlistItem). A student counts once per course.
    • A course is short of listings when it has interested students and nothing on sale, or at least 2 interested students per available listing. The gap is demand minus supply.
