    How this is calculated

    • A meetup is a meeting proposal with status ACCEPTED: the place and time both students agreed in the chat.
    • Only meetups whose end time has passed have an outcome. The ones still to come are counted apart as upcoming.
    • Completed: the buyer of that chat completed the exchange of that listing. Cancelled: the exchange was cancelled and never completed. Not closed: no exchange was completed or cancelled, so the meetup was missed or never confirmed in the app.
    • Changes before agreeing are the proposals of the same chat that were declined or withdrawn before the accepted one. They are counted over every agreed meetup, upcoming ones included.
    • The hour is the start of the meetup in campus time ({{ campus_time_zone }}).
