Insights

    How this is calculated

    • Only ratings with a written review are classified. Ratings with stars and tags only are counted in the total.
    • Each review is lower-cased and accents are removed. Every word from a positive list adds 1 and every word from a negative list subtracts 1 (English and Spanish). A negation right before a word (not good, no amable) flips it.
    • A total above 0 is positive, below 0 is negative, and 0 is neutral.
    • Keywords are the most frequent words of 3 letters or more in each group, without common words like the or muy.
    • Agreement with stars compares the text with the stars (4–5 positive, 3 neutral, 1–2 negative). A word list misses sarcasm and context, so treat the result as indicative.
