    How this is calculated

    • Every listing (Material) counts, whatever its status. Faculty comes from the seller's profile (User.faculty).
    • A field is empty when it is null, only blank spaces, or (for photos) an empty list.
    • Edition only applies to books and model only to calculators and lab equipment. Listings of other categories are not counted for those fields, so a calculator without an edition is not a gap.
    • The empty rate is empty listings divided by the listings the field applies to. A complete listing has every field that applies to it filled in.
