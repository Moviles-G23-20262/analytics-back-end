Insights

    How this is calculated

    • Every listing is placed in the group of its seller's average stars (from all the ratings the seller received) and, apart, in the group of how many reviews the seller has.
    • A listing is contacted when at least one buyer opened a chat about it. Buyers per listing counts distinct buyers.
    • A listing is sold when its exchange was completed or its status is SOLD. Days to sell goes from publishing to the completed exchange.
    • The grouping uses the seller's reputation today, not the one they had when the listing was published, and a small group can swing a lot: treat the result as indicative until each group has enough listings.
