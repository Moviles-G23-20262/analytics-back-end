Insights

    How this is calculated

    • Listings are all materials in the category, whatever their status.
    • Completed exchanges are the exchanges with status COMPLETED for those materials. Pending and cancelled exchanges are not counted.
    • Exchange rate is completed exchanges divided by listings.
    • Rates of categories with few listings swing widely; check the listing count before drawing conclusions.
