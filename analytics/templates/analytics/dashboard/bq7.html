Insights

    How this is calculated

    • A wishlist item is one buyer saving one listing. Buyers counts distinct users with at least one saved item.
    • An item received a Smart Match when that buyer got a SMART_MATCH notification for that listing. The first one is the reference time.
    • Bought after the Smart Match: the same buyer completed the exchange for that listing after the notification. Conversion rate is this count divided by the items that received a Smart Match.
    • Bought without a Smart Match: the item was purchased and never received one.
    • The comparison between the two rates is observational. Items that get a Smart Match may differ from the others in ways this data does not capture, so it does not prove that the notification caused the purchase.
