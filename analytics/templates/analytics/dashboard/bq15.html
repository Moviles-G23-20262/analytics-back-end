    How this is calculated

    • A sale is counted when its exchange has status COMPLETED. Pending and cancelled exchanges are not counted.
    • The ticket is Exchange.price, in Colombian pesos. Each exchange is joined to its material to know the item condition.
    • The overall average is the total of all completed sales divided by the number of sales, so a condition with many sales weighs more than one with few.
    • Sales whose material has no condition count in the overall average but not in any condition bar.
    • Averages are rounded to whole pesos.
