    How this is calculated

    • An item is sold when its exchange has status COMPLETED. Pending and cancelled exchanges are not counted.
    • Time to sell is Exchange.completedAt minus Material.createdAt, in days with decimals (12 hours = 0.5 days).
    • Exchanges without a completion date, or completed before the listing was created (seeded or edited data), are skipped and counted apart.
    • The average is pulled up by a few slow sales; the median is the typical case.
