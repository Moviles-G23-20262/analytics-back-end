Insights

    How this is calculated

    • Publishing is the creation time of each listing. Browsing is every LISTING_VIEW and SEARCH event.
    • The endpoint groups by weekday and hour in UTC. The dashboard shifts the hour (and the weekday across midnight) by the campus offset, taken from {{ campus_time_zone }}. Choose UTC to see the data exactly as the endpoint returns it.
    • There are far more browsing events than published listings, so the default view shows each series as a share of its own total. This compares when activity happens, not how much. Switch to raw counts to see volumes.
    • Parts of the day: night 00:00–05:59, morning 06:00–11:59, afternoon 12:00–17:59, evening 18:00–23:59.
